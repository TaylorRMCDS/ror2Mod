# Seeker

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Seeker]`. Available toggles: `EnableUtilityChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Utility


- Reprieve: Flight speed is reduced by 10%. Turning starts at normal strength and decreases linearly with speed to 80% lower at maximum flight speed.

## Special


- Saving Grace: Keeps its +7% stat bonus and Meditate's ally effects. After surviving damage at 25% combined health or lower, Seeker consumes the charge to heal 75% maximum health instantly. It no longer self-revives. The once-per-stage consumption limit remains.
- Palm Blast: Reaching seven Tranquility grants Saving Grace to living survivors. It does not gain Meditate's dead-ally revive.
