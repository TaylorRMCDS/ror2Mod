# Huntress

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Huntress]`. Available toggles: `EnableUtilityChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Utility


- Blink: Base cooldown reduced from 7 to 6 seconds.

## Special


- Arrow Rain: Damage increased by 5%. The native proc coefficient is unchanged.
- Arrow Rain: Falling arrows now fill the extended damage column.
- Arrow Rain: Vertical reach extended to 100 meters above the targeted area, including through intervening platforms. Horizontal radius is unchanged.
