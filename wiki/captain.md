# Captain

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Captain]`. Available toggles: `EnableSecondaryChanges`, `EnableUtilityChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Secondary


- Power Tazer: Shock-immune enemies are slowed by 50% for 5 seconds.

## Utility


- Orbital Probe and OGM-72 'DIABLO' Strike: Can be used in hidden realms that normally block orbital skills. Stock, targeting and delivery rules remain.

## Special


- Orbital Supply Beacon: Can be used in hidden realms that normally block orbital skills. Normal stock, targeting and delivery rules remain.
- Healing Beacon: Allies already at full combined health receive barrier from each normal healing tick, accumulating up to 20% of maximum health. Vanilla grants no barrier. Each tick grants up to 2% maximum health every 0.2 seconds, limited by the remaining barrier cap. Shields count toward the full-health check; a tick that finishes healing does not also grant barrier. Existing barrier is never removed or reduced by this cap.
