# CHEF

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[CHEF]`. Available toggles: `EnableSecondaryChanges`, `EnableUtilityChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Secondary


- Ice Box: Each cast randomly selects one straight cube; the other two each have slight independent random pitch and yaw within a 2.5-degree cone.
- Boosted Ice Box: Damage reduced from 1500% to 1000%.
## Utility


- Roll: Automatic step climbing is disabled during Roll, with a 60-degree slope limit (or the original motor limit if lower). Steep collisions and ramp crests cannot create upward launch speed. Deliberate jumps remain available; normal motor settings return on exit.

## Special


- Yes CHEF: Can be used during Roll without ending it.
