# False Son

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[False Son]`. Available toggles: `EnablePassiveChanges`, `EnableUtilityChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Passive


- Lunar Tampering: Unused spikes grant 4% attack speed and 2 armor each; used spikes grant 4% movement speed and 2 health regeneration each.

## Utility


- Step of the Brothers: The zero-damage detection sweep cannot proc items. Explosions retain their normal damage and proc coefficient.

## Special


- Laser of the Father: Hits refresh existing Lunar Ruin timers and apply one stack every 24 consecutive ticks per enemy (about 3 seconds at base attack speed). Misses and new casts reset streaks.
- Lunar Ruin: Bonus damage no longer multiplies the shared damage packet, and Ruin counts only once toward Death Mark.
- Laser Burst: Its 50% Lunar Spike refill rounds up, including one-stock loadouts.
- Laser of the Father: Beam visuals update every rendered frame.
