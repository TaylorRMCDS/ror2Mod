# Commando

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Commando]`. Available toggles: `EnableSecondaryChanges`, `EnableUtilityChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Secondary


- Phase Round: Initial damage increased from 300% to 320%. Each enemy pierced now adds 80% of initial damage, up from vanilla's 40%. The existing additive ramp now deals 320%, 576%, 832%, and so on, before other modifiers.
- Phase Blast: Each pellet's proc coefficient increased from 0.5 to 0.7. The two blasts still fire 4 pellets each at 200% damage per pellet.

## Utility


- Tactical Dive: Adds 200 armor for 0.5 seconds, using the standard visible ArmorBoost buff. It does not stack another armor bonus onto an active ArmorBoost buff.

## Special


- Suppressive Fire: Base shot count increased from 6 to 8, and damage per bullet increased from 100% to 115%. Shot count still increases with attack speed.
- Frag Grenade: Outgoing speed after enemy collisions reduced by 50%. Ground and wall bounces, damage, fuse and blast radius are unchanged.
