# MUL-T

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[MUL-T]`. Available toggles: `EnableStatsChanges`, `EnablePrimaryChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Primary


- Scrap Launcher: Reload speed scales with attack speed.
- Scrap Launcher: Explosions now deal full damage throughout their existing blast radius, with no distance falloff or sweet spot.
- Power-Saw: Direct saw kills grant 0.5s of Regenerative. Nonlethal hits, DOT kills and item-proc kills do not trigger it. Works in either Power Mode slot.

## Stats


- Base acceleration increased from 30 to 60. Movement speed is unchanged.
