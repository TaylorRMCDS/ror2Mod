# Loader

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Loader]`. Available toggles: `EnableStatsChanges`, `EnableSecondaryChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Secondary


- Grapple Fist: Halves cooldown after 1 second or less attached. Flight time does not count; misses and Spiked Fist receive no refund.

## Stats


- Base maximum health reduced from 160 to 130. Health growth remains +48 per level.
- Base armor reduced from 20 to 10.
