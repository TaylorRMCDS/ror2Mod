# Bandit

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Bandit]`. Available toggles: `EnablePrimaryChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Primary


- Blast: Ammo capacity increased from 4 to 6 rounds, with 6 matching reticle pips.
- Blast: Minimum interval between successful shots set to 0.3 seconds, independent of attack speed, reloads and skill interrupts. Vanilla uses a 0.1-second minimum base duration that scales with attack speed; the added elapsed-time limit prevents firing sooner than 0.3 seconds apart.
- Blast: Firing spread and bloom buildup reduced by 50%.
- Blast: Bullet radius increased by 20%; pierces enemies and stops at terrain.
- Blast: Reticle pips remain centered when switching between Blast's 6-round display and Burst's 4-round display.
