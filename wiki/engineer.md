# Engineer

T4ylorSurvivors 1.1.0 survivor changes.

Config section: `[Engineer]`. Available toggles: `EnableStatsChanges`, `EnablePrimaryChanges`, `EnableSecondaryChanges`, `EnableSpecialChanges`. Restart after editing; use matching settings across multiplayer peers.

## Primary


- Bouncing Grenades: Base charge rate increased by 10%. Charge timing is divided by 1.10 (about 90.91% of vanilla duration), not reduced by 10%. The native 3-second total duration becomes about 2.727 seconds, and the 2-second maximum charge time becomes about 1.818 seconds, before attack-speed scaling. Maximum grenade count and grenade damage are preserved.

## Secondary


- Pressure Mines: Fully armed damage increased from 900% to 1000%. Weak and partially armed damage are unchanged.

## Special


- TR58 Carbonizer Turret: Base health regeneration increased from 0.6 to 2.0 health per second. Base health remains 130; health growth is unchanged.

## Stats


- Survivor base damage reduced from 14 to 13, and damage growth reduced from +2.8 to +2.6 per level.
